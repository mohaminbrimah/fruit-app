About FreshHarvest

From orchard to your table — our passion is bringing you the best nature has to offer.

Our Story

FreshHarvest started with a simple belief: every Ghanaian deserves access to fruits that taste as good as they look. What began as a small stand near the University of Ghana has grown into a trusted destination for families who value quality, freshness, and honest local farming.

We work directly with growers across Ghana — from the Eastern Region's pineapple fields to Volta banana farms. Every mango, pawpaw, and plantain is hand-selected and delivered at peak flavor.

Whether you're packing school lunches or hosting a summer brunch, we're here to make healthy eating delicious and effortless.
